An entry for the [One Line of Earned Pride](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557) bounty on make.fun: one original line, 1–240 characters, a quiet, earned moment of pride for someone in a scene.

## The line

> For forty years she cleaned the library after closing; tonight the librarian handed her the key and said, “Lock up when you’re ready” — and she was.

Forty years of invisible work, and the pride arrives not as applause but as trust: the key, handed over. The last three words land it — she was ready all along.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (stdlib only) validates the line in `entry.txt` is 1–240 characters, then copies it byte for byte to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (or `/output/entry.txt` when that variable is unset), verifying the output is byte-identical.
